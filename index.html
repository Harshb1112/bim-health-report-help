❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my reports saved?

All reports are automatically saved to your Desktop folder with a timestamp in the filename.

Do I need an internet connection?

No! The plugin works completely offline. No login or internet connection required.

What report formats are generated?

Three formats: HTML (interactive charts), DOCX (Word document), and JSON (raw data).

Can I generate PDF reports?

Yes! Open the HTML report in any browser and use "Print to PDF" to save as PDF.

How long does report generation take?

Typically 5-30 seconds depending on model size and complexity.
